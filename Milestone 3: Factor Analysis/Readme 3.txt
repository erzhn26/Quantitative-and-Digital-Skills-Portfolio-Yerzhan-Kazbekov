Construction Cost Overrun Risk Analysis
Skills Demonstrated
Factor Analysis: Reduced 44 risk variables → 6 latent factors (26.6% variance explained; KMO=0.767)

Regression Modeling: R²=0.223; identified supply/procurement issues (β=0.172) and contract management (β=0.080) as top cost overrun drivers

Risk Scoring: Created composite risk scores; segmented 143 respondents into 5 tiers

Advanced Excel: Matrix operations (MMULT, MINVERSE), array formulas, correlation analysis

Key Finding
Experience is the strongest protective factor (β=-0.207) against cost overruns.
